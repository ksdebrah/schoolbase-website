# PupilBook website

The public website for PupilBook, school management for basic schools in Ghana.

- Three designs of the same site: A at the root (`index.html`), B in `b/`, C in `c/`. Each is one self-contained page with its own `fonts/` (OFL) and `img/`.
- Product pictures (MacBook `d-`, iPhone `p-`, iPad `t-`, and each sample school `c-`) are screenshots of the PupilBook app taken from made-up sample schools (PupilBook Academy in PupilBook colours, Sunrise School in green, Bright Future School in wine), never a real school. Photos are from Unsplash under the Unsplash License, credited on the page.
- The sources live in the private PupilBook repository (`website/`, `website-b/`, `website-c/`); pictures are retaken with its `tools/siteshots.py`.
- Served by GitHub Pages from the `main` branch.
- Contact numbers go in the `CONTACT` object near the end of `index.html`.
- "Sign in" links go to the PupilBook portal.
