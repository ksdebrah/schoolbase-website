Questions schools ask

Do we need computers?

No. SchoolBase works in the web browser on any phone, tablet or computer. Most teachers use their own phones. A printer helps for receipts and report cards, but you can also share them by WhatsApp.

Our internet is not reliable. Will it still work?

Yes. Registers, payments and scores save on the phone when the network drops and send by themselves when it comes back. The screen always shows whether everything has been sent.

Can we keep our own report card layout?

Yes. Send us a copy of your current report card and we set yours up to match, with your crest, colours, grading and comment banks.

How long does it take to start?

Setting up a school takes about a day once we have your lists. We recommend going live at the start of a term, with two weeks of training for the head teacher, bursar and one class teacher.

Can parents see their child's results?

Parents get texts for receipts, absences and reminders, and report cards can be sent to them by WhatsApp. A parent app is planned.

What if we need help?

Every page has a help button, every role has its own user guide, and you can chat with our support team from inside SchoolBase or on WhatsApp.
